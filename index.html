Case Study 03 · Junior NetSuite Analyst

Warehouse ERP & RF-SMART Integration

Pride Truck Sales · Aug 2023 – Jan 2024
Challenge

The warehouse team was losing trust in the system over three recurring problems: inventory quantities in NetSuite didn't match physical stock counts, inventory showed as on-hand but wasn't actually available to pick, and RF-SMART barcode scans sometimes returned the wrong item or quantity.

What I Did

Investigated and corrected stock discrepancies through NetSuite Inventory Adjustments and Transfer Orders, traced pick-availability issues back to bin, location, and status settings, and worked with warehouse staff and IT to troubleshoot RF-SMART scanning errors integrated with NetSuite WMS — testing each fix through UAT before rollout and documenting the resolution process for the team.

Outcome

More accurate inventory counts, fewer picking delays, and fewer scan-related errors — restoring the warehouse and accounting teams' confidence in NetSuite as the source of truth for stock.

Case Study 04 · PS Operations Analyst · Fictionalized Example

NetSuite Job Creation & Project Setup Optimization

Oracle NetSuite Corporation · Mar 2018 – Feb 2020
Challenge

A newly signed engagement — ABC Corporation, an ERP Implementation project — hit a billing/subsidiary mismatch shortly after the Job was set up in NetSuite. The subsidiary on the Job record didn't match the signed Sales Order, putting revenue allocation, invoicing, and financial reporting at risk if it reached go-live uncorrected.

What I Did

Compared the Job record against the signed SOW and Sales Order to trace the mismatch back to the subsidiary field, then coordinated with Sales, Finance, and Project Management to confirm the correct subsidiary, billing configuration, and financial classification. Corrected the Job record directly in NetSuite and re-validated it against the SOW before releasing it for time entry, billing, and reporting.

Outcome

Prevented incorrect revenue allocation and delayed invoicing on the engagement, and the pre-activation checklist built from this fix now helps Sales and Deal Desk catch similar subsidiary mismatches earlier in the handoff, before they reach Job setup.
